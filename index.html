Who's behind it

Owner-operated, one block at a time.

The Rolling Root is run by Debbie Lee, who lives within walking distance of the Public Square. Same person plants your containers, sources your tropicals, and shows up to water them — there's no crew, no dispatcher, and no account manager to call.

It started as a rolling cart of tropical houseplants for people who'd otherwise drive to Atlanta. The storefront accounts followed, because once one shop on the block looked tended, the ones beside it wanted the same.

Let the beauty sell itself.
